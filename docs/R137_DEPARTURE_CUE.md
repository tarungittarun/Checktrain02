The tracking table now distinguishes a reported **departure state** from a measured position. An admitted official departure receives its actual departure minute in the source column, and a symbolic icon a quarter of the way towards the first *validated mapped point* (otherwise the next halt). The fraction is visual only: the rail does not paint a travelled-distance segment and accessibility does not call it progress or GPS. The last halt keeps its dot rather than a second locomotive. The folded leg prints `Between Birur Jn and Sivapur · departure reported; position unknown` when Sivapur is present in the validated map; expanding the station list hides that redundant sentence on the same tap. Without a matching map, it says the next scheduled halt instead. Old reports say `Last departure ... current segment unknown` rather than asserting the train is still on that leg. No departure, a schedule-only row or a comparison-only page cannot earn the cue.

The selected `.org.in` page's **last card** may offer a revisable departure minute. Earlier 20689 captures saw the card's departure change with the page clock; the parser intentionally keeps it out of site-reported actuals. If it separately passes existing past-card/schedule-pair checks, follows an admitted arrival, is at least two minutes older than the page publication, is no more than ten minutes old, and the page is within five minutes of this check, the screen may show an **amber, unconfirmed website departure suggestion**, its minute with a `?`, `WEB?` and `not actual` accessibility. The symbolic icon remains only a suggestion. This does not fill `Act`, establish today's origin date (the site's run is inferred), adopt the Google phone application's distance, or certify movement. If the page contains no candidate departure or fails those gates, TrackTrain remains at the last admitted arrival; auto-refresh is not itself evidence. On a subsequent report at a new intermediate/halt the corresponding source event takes precedence. Selected-source/fallback attribution is unchanged.
