# R133 · Tracking presentation and connection failure cue

- Collapsed intermediate leg: a source-named site point may identify its `At …` and `As on …` comparison beside the locomotive. Expanding the mapped stations hides those two nearby captions; the expanded rail shows the station labels instead. The logo and event-age indication remain where the underlying source supports them. This does **not** promote website comparisons to primary ACTUAL or current position (R132 admission policy remains).
- Delay ink: positive reported lateness is red; explicitly early is green (`early`: daylight `#137442`, night `#65DA92`); on-time is neutral blue-gray (`delay_neutral`: daylight `#315A78`, night `#B9D6E9`); unavailable data keeps its warning tone. Palette tests require WCAG AA 4.5:1 contrast against each summary surface. No green for on-time or positive delay, including a small positive mirror delay.
- A **classified transport failure**, not an unanswered actual-time field, puts a persistent, source-named reason at the top of the summary (also when collapsed). Its card briefly dips in opacity at three pulses/second for two seconds, with a small one-time horizontal shake. The card never flashes white/black indefinitely. A new attempt clears the old warning; success or selecting another working source clears it; leaving the screen stops animation. The previous train event is not re-aged, moved, or replaced by a failure message. HTTP 403/429 is labelled blocked, timeout is labelled timed out, other connection failure is labelled failed.
- Coverage: `InlineRouteRenderTest` paints collapsed/expanded clock/name variants; `ConnectionCueAndDelayTest` asserts the palette, timings and persistent warning lifecycle; `RefreshFlashTest` distinguishes source refusal, timeout and answer-without-actuals. Android CI validates compiled APK/JVM/render/installed-APK partitions after this change. E2E was **not requested** for R133 and is not triggered by this work. Passing device automation remains distinct from the owner's own phone check.
