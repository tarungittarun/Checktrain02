# R153 · the badge and headline must be visible at the first glance

The R152 published-APK offline/journey acceptance still failed even after the failure warning stopped replacing the source badge. Its device-side geometry (reported without relaxing the acceptance assertion) was conclusive: `badge='SCHEDULE · no verified actuals'`, 75px high, `details=35/656`, card 577px, footer ending at 533px. The **inner report viewport had been made smaller than the badge** to keep all pinned footer controls inside the outer source cap. On that narrow/420-dpi emulator, a long next-stop line consumed almost the entire allowed card. This was a layout priority error, not missing provenance data.

R153 bounds the card against the route as before, but its own height must be at least `measured pinned chrome + measured badge/warning + headline`. The outer source card can scroll to reach pinned Delay, source chooser and Auto if that combination exceeds the route-preserving budget; it now has a persistent scrollbar in that case. The inner report viewport also has a scrollbar for further site notices/notes. On ordinary phones, all lines fit naturally with neither blank reserved alert band nor content layered behind Delay. The failed-check badge stays visible alongside its warning (R152). The CI unit case records the *observed 577/542/75 pixel geometry* so it cannot shrink back to 35px.

This is a presentation-only fix. Source claims, timed event, route marker and selected-source refresh remain unchanged. Automatic Android CI and published-APK acceptance must still pass; the separate manual E2E workflow stays disabled until explicitly requested. PR #3 stays open pending real-phone confirmation.
