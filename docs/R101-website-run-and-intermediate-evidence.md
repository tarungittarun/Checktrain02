# 20689 website run/date, station clocks and route position (15 Oct 2026)

These are **read-only observations**, not proof of a train's physical location. Do not confuse `whereismytrain.org.in` with Google's Where is my Train phone app (which advertises onboard cell-tower location). The website's own FAQ claims IRCTC/NTES APIs, but the train page does not disclose its underlying live event feed. Its one embedded `fetch('/api/search.php?q=…&type=…')` is **search autocomplete**, not a dated running-status or kilometre feed. The three occurrences of “distance” on the 20689 HTML are SEO/promotional descriptions, not a train-distance reading. [Trial at commit c6348bf](https://github.com/tarungittarun/Checktrain02/commit/c6348bf), [request-code trial at 8bbf70b](https://github.com/tarungittarun/Checktrain02/commit/8bbf70b).

## Run-day association, not a site-supplied run date

`/?trainNo=20689` identifies train and endpoints but **does not show an origin date**. Fetching `/?trainNo=20689&date=2026-10-14` at 14:02 and 14:04 IST on 15 October produced the **same visible station cards** as the undated URL. The size/hash changed because the request was echoed, not because the returned journey changed. The app therefore accepts these claims only while the selected origin timetable has one active plausible run: train number, endpoints and scheduled weekday must match; elapsed time must lie between origin departure and the bounded end of that run. This rules out historical, future and ambiguous daily runs; it does **not** prove which run the site used. An NTES same-day report matching a station clock within two minutes supports association with the selected dated journey, but may share an upstream feed with the site. The app must never call that independent verification of the site's provenance.

## Page update versus station movement

At ~14:02:02, the page said “Last Updated 14:01:30”; at ~14:02:48 it said 14:02:46, **without any change to the ten reported station-code groups**. At ~14:04:12 the page marked SME (Shivamogga) past with 14:03 arrival/14:04 departure; at ~14:04:58 the page clock advanced again and none of its station cards changed. An HTTP success or recent page clock establishes **page freshness**, not train movement. The separate arrival/departure clocks in past-marked green station cards are **site-published claims**, not independently verified actuals. Upcoming white cards may contain a first clock that is a forecast: they must not fill Act cells.

The owner's diary shows BDVT website departure progressing 13:47→13:48→13:51→13:52 on successive refreshes while the last reached station remained BDVT. Therefore the **latest past card's departure is provisional**; R101 omits it from reported departure/position until another station becomes past. Earlier stations' departures can remain site-attributed past clocks. The receipt displays HTTP response seconds, the page-clock age and the latest station-event age separately. An arrival delay is computed only against the published schedule; it is labelled **site arrival delay**, not measured delay. BDVT site arrival 13:42 vs 12:50 schedule implies site +52 minutes; an NTES dated BDVT arrival 13:43 implies NTES +53 minutes. The one-minute agreement is useful cross-checking, but not proof the two publishers used independent sensors.

## Intermediate points and distance

The 20689 website past-marked BEI (Shivamogga Bidare) around 13:59/14:01 and then SME (Shivamogga) around 14:03/14:04. These are intermediate stations **between scheduled halts BDVT and SMET**; a halt-only parser misses them. If an independently fetched/static, train-matched route lists one of these stations **in order and with credible kilometre distances**, R101 can draw an amber locomotive *at that named station's static-map fraction of the BDVT–SMET leg*, with the station-event age and site attribution. This does **not** locate the train anywhere after the station, extrapolate from wall time, measure route km from the website or confer GPS certainty. No valid static map, unmatched route, missing station, implausible schedule clock, stale page or white/upcoming card means **no intermediate marker**. The full station table still carries only independently available static names/distances for other intermediate points. This policy does not claim that the site succeeds on every train.

## Requests / repeatability

The read-only probe lives at `tools/ci/probe_org_website.py`; the workflow `.github/workflows/org-source-research.yml` comments bounded results on its commit. The 20689 observations are a specific run on 15 October, not a universal service guarantee. Additional phone trials should compare the current *selected origin day* with an NTES dated run and keep actual/event timestamps separate from source/publication clocks.
