TRAIN APP TEST — 4 minutes, Android only

Works on Android 7.0 and newer. Install (about 10.6 MB). On the page, confirm the size says 10,591,177 bytes - if it is smaller you have
an old build. Tap the file and choose "Install anyway" if the phone warns you. No account, no login, no ads.

1. Type three letters in each box: "Bang" -> pick KSR Bengaluru, "Chenn" -> pick MGR Chennai Central.
2. Tap -1d, now and +1d once each. The date and the list must change.
3. Tap "Find trains". On a train that is running right now (leaving in the next hour or two is best), tap "Track".
4. Look at the pill under the big headline.
   - "LIVE · running status" plus a delay  ->  working, that is what we want to see.
   - "OFFLINE · schedule only"              ->  live data did not reach your phone. That is the bug. Tell us.
5. Check the stop list: upcoming stops must show a time marked "projected" or "reported", or a dash with the
   word "timetable". A dash is honest. A wrong delay is not - screenshot it if you see one.
6. Compare with NTES or RailYatri for the same train at the same minute. Note the difference in minutes.
7. Turn on airplane mode and search again. It must still work (timetables are inside the app).

IF THE PILL SAYS OFFLINE (1 minute, this is the part that actually helps):
8. Back -> "Data source" -> under Diagnostic log tap "Turn on collection". The line should say "Collecting".
9. Open the same train again, wait 40 seconds, tap "Refresh" once.
10. Back -> "Data source" -> tap "Share" and send it to me. (Or "Save to file" and forward the file.)
11. Also, in the same minute: open enquiry.indianrail.gov.in/ntes in Chrome on that phone. Does it load?
12. Then turn Wi-Fi OFF, keep mobile data on, tap "Refresh" once. Does the pill change?
