# R130 · floating Refresh and explicit Find · 18 Oct 2026

- [x] Tracking Refresh moved out of the status card into a **48dp floating control** over the lower screen. Its default centre is **7/8 of content width** from the left and **4/5 of content height** from the top (1/8 from the right; 1/5 above the bottom). The control can be dragged and dropped within the **lower half**, stays off the outer edges, and saves its relative position across reopen/rotation. Tap and accessibility click still run the existing selected-source-first refresh; dragging does not make a request. Only the 28dp glyph rotates when busy.

Selected website text is shortened in the narrow control; the expanded choice still names the website as unverified.
